  (ok), waits until Ben's first claim has landed, then claims target 0 again (taken), and checks the round's end.
- Lines tagged [ana] are Ana's. Every other line is the bot's. Both processes append to one file, so the lines
  are in the order they happened.

Main run result: the bot's two claims were answered ok (r5 for target 1, r6 for target 2) and the bot left after
its one round, with exit 0. Ana's claim on her own find (target 0) was answered taken. The round ended all-found.
The bot never claims a found target: it skips them from the newest snapshot, so a taken answer for the bot
only happens in a race, where its snapshot lags a find by one broadcast.
